### Root causes addressed

- **Home invalidations were not diagnosable.** The supplied `Pasted text(20261028-061806).txt` reports `on_enter_snapshot_invalidated` without the failing snapshot condition, so that exact historical invalidation cannot be assigned to a particular branch. The source path could reject a cached frame on visual/layout key or page mismatch; Lite three-row slot changes could not be safely repaired after a move because the prior snapshot stored slot identities and visual keys without the saved face rectangles. The new logs name the failing condition. Snapshot format v11 stores the 42 small slot rectangles, retains readers for versions 8–10, checks the current visual signature and page, then clears old rectangles before drawing any changed/moved faces. It patches progress/status, reordered or newly visible books, selected details, and statistics through the existing framebuffer and idle-only SD snapshot writer. Global artwork presentation changes participate in the visual signature; per-book cover/metadata changes are detected by the slot visual key and redraw only affected faces when saved geometry allows. - **Modal close forced a slow panel waveform.** `ActivityManager` requested `FULL_REFRESH` for every modal overlay dismissal, even when the normal render path had restored a clean Home frame. Home visual identity includes theme, layout, orientation, language, spine decoration, featured fields, and other displayed settings, while Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and reader-font preferences do not invalidate Home. This is separate from settings persistence: the supplied log already reports `save_result=unchanged elapsed_us=9299 bytes=2522`.
- **Home drawing and e-paper waveform were coupled.** Ordinary restore, patch, and overlay-close frames use the fast waveform. The existing refresh-frequency choices still schedule full ghost cleanup after 1, 5, 10, 15, or 30 Home updates; an orientation change also gets a full resync. Each full update now logs its reason. A periodic cleanup remains enabled.
- **BLE could restart during the same measured render-pressure period that caused a survival stop.** Reader BLE return now backs off from 15 seconds up to 120 seconds after repeated survival stops. The stop history resets after 60 seconds without another stop, after three consecutive renders with at least 36 KiB current and recent-minimum free heap plus a 20 KiB largest block, and when the Reader exits/re-enters. The existing global 96 KiB free / 36 KiB largest-block start floors and the narrower quiescent EPUB-return policy are unchanged.
