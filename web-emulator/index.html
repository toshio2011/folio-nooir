Folio Nooir · v1.6.6 port work

A real firmware emulator, with Nooir’s reading experience.

This page wraps the pinned eink-qemu WebAssembly engine. It runs the selected firmware image in your browser and provides the virtual screen, device controls, serial console, and FAT32 SD-card tools.

Local by design. Firmware files and custom .bin selections stay in your browser. This project does not upload firmware to the public eink-qemu demo or to a Nooir server.

Emulation engine

eink-qemu

Built from serialx/eink-qemu at d1b24a5fbc1bbae7ea31075460f63cd4c989c1dc. QEMU 11.1.2 and its ESP32 models are GPL-2.0; the upstream tree also includes file-specific LGPL and MIT components. Its notices and source are required in any packaged distribution.

Emulator support assets

Espressif and Emscripten

The C3 ROM support image is converted from Espressif's Apache-2.0 ESP32-C3 rev3 ROM ELF. Emscripten 6.0.12 compiles the engine and supplies its SDL2 browser port.
